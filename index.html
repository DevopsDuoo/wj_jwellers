Four Decades of Haute Horlogerie & High Jewelry

1. Artisanal Metallurgy

Hand-alloyed 22K (916) and 18K (750) precious golds smelted in induction crucibles, achieving a signature rich warm hue with unmatched ductility for intricate filigree.

Zero Porosity Casting

2. Syndicate Polki & GIA

We select exclusively flat-bottomed natural uncut diamonds with high transparency and fire, backed by 24K pure gold foil lac-free jadau techniques.

Flawless Faceting

Heritage Enameling

Every creation carries a laser-engraved serial code, archival passport certificate, and complimentary lifetime ultrasonic cleaning and valuation updates.
